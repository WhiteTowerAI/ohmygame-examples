## Player-Visible Changes

- Persistent combat information: HP, gold, relics, deck, enemy intents, Energy, and Heat. Resources and statuses use icons plus values.
- Removed repeated scene titles, the hero's duplicate health bar, persistent tutorials, text lists of powers, and confirmation buttons when unnecessary.
- Essential actions are **Vent** and **End Turn**. Selecting a non-targeted card shows **Play**; after selecting an Attack, click the enemy to confirm.
- Click Heat, relics, and powers for details. Settings retains the handbook and battle log. At Heat 6, show the end-turn HP cost explicitly.
- Sixteen newly generated copper-furnace icons, a text-free card frame, and three button plates have been sliced and integrated. Text, costs, effects, and live values remain programmatically typeset; the first iteration used Chinese.
- Mobile uses a horizontal hand and fixed bottom bar. At 360×640, pile controls move above the battlefield so they do not obscure cards; action targets are at least 44 pixels.

## Current Boundaries

The Chapter I core loop is implemented and key paths have been checked separately. A continuous manual clear of all twelve floors on a normal random route and long-term balance validation are not complete. The compendium, permanent chapter records, and expanded route display on the results screen are not implemented.

Additional enemies currently reuse four existing designs. Relic slices have not been extracted pixel by pixel. Characters use static sprites and short movement effects. Audio currently consists only of synthesized action effects, with no complete music, voice acting, or volume sliders.

## Project Entry Points

`src/main.ts` owns UI and input; `src/engine.ts` owns serializable rules; `src/data.ts` defines content; `src/world.ts` owns Phaser character presentation. HUD styles are in `src/hud.css`. `npm test` runs rule checks and `npm run build` produces the statically publishable `dist/`. Runtime artwork is already integrated; this example omits the original asset-regeneration scripts.

## English Edition and Notification Styling

At the user's request, the game display language became English and the title became **Ember Route**. This covers all cards, statuses, relics, enemies, menus, map, rewards, services, events, results, handbook, battle log, and accessibility labels. At that iteration, the main design stayed in Chinese, as did internal rule tags; content IDs and the v1 save format remained stable. No rules or values changed. This section supersedes the earlier Chinese UI/font direction without deleting historical verification records. The canvas documents have since been translated into English as well; internal rule tags remain untouched.

Notifications changed from a green plate to charcoal metal, a thin copper border, warm-white text, and a small diamond. New notifications replace old ones rather than stacking, and normally fade after about 3.2 seconds. Detail windows use matching dark copper borders and retain reduced-motion support. English card names/titles use Georgia/Times New Roman, with system sans-serif body text. The embedded Chinese font is no longer loaded or bundled. Long card names, descriptions, and mobile modal spacing were adapted; the deck remains two columns at 360×640.

On Continue, old Chinese battle logs are converted to English, with an English fallback for unknown records. New automated checks cover English content and legacy-log restoration, confirming that saved state outside the logs is unchanged. All 14 tests and the build passed. Publish checks confirmed English HTML, 40 art files, and exclusion of the DEV bridge/fixed scenarios and embedded Chinese font.

Desktop input reduced enemy HP 32→9, then recovered a card and killed the enemy. Mobile touch rewards increased deck size 52→53, followed map → event → shop, and checked English recovery modals, settings, and handbook. At 360×640, fatal Overheat, main menu, and New Journey worked. A normal notification disappeared after 3.7 seconds. Desktop notification/long-text hand and mobile short/long notifications, reward, deck, and main-menu screenshots were reviewed in that iteration. Auditing 52 normal/upgraded deck cards found no title/description overflow; a short-screen hand title issue was fixed, and there was no horizontal page overflow. `scenario=cards` extends notifications to 15 seconds only in the isolated DEV screenshot scenario; production remains at 3.2 seconds. No game-script errors or failed asset requests were observed; sessions were closed.

Every event/relic and all twelve random floors have not been manually replayed individually. Continuous dragging, independent sound review, and balance-validation limits remain unchanged.
